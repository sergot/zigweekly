# Contributing

Thank you for helping. The newsletter is assembled from pull requests.

## Add a link

1. Open the current file in `draft/` (there is one at a time).
2. Add one line under the right section, in this form:

   ```md
   - [Title](https://primary-source.example) — one plain sentence about it.
   ```

3. One link per pull request keeps reviews quick and merges conflict-free.

Sections: Language & compiler, ZLS, Community projects, Articles & videos,
Call for participation, Upcoming events, Jobs.

## Rules

- Link to the primary source (the project, the author's blog, the official issue), not a repost.
- Articles and other content written by an LLM are not accepted.
- Keep it factual and short: one sentence, no hype.
- Events: include the date and city, and only events that have not happened yet.
- Do not add anything private, such as content from closed chats.
- Credit people by the name they use publicly in the post you are linking.

## Removal and corrections

If something about you or your project should be removed or credited differently,
open an issue or pull request, or email hello@zigweekly.com. We will act on it quickly.

## Publishing

The editor moves the finished draft from `draft/` to `issues/` in a pull request and merges it.
Merging to `main` deploys the site. Please do not move files out of `draft/` yourself.
